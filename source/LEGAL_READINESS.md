# FitMyStyle 9.0 — production legal & tailoring readiness

**Status: prototype / not ready to accept orders.** This is a software release guide, not legal advice, conformity certification or an approved pattern library.

## Sizing and production
- Body circumference, garment circumference, designed ease and seam allowance are separate quantities. ISO 8559-1:2017 provides anthropometric definitions; this app uses an illustrative subset, not a certified ISO implementation.
- The procedural 3D mannequin represents panel, sleeve and finishing geometry only. It does **not** solve cloth physics, predict actual fit, or generate a production-cuttable 3D-derived pattern.
- Before commercial production, require material composition and shrinkage tests, graded supplier size charts, stitching specifications, garment construction review, sewn sample and fitting approval.
- For children, review drawstring and entrapment risks against EN 14682; this preview intentionally omits hood cords on child presets.

## Consumer law
- EU distance contracts usually carry a 14-day withdrawal right; personalised/made-to-specification goods can qualify for an exception, but defect rights remain. See https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm
- Activate checkout only after publishing seller identity, delivery/returns terms, transparent full prices (including taxes and shipment), complaint process and legally compliant terms.

## Privacy
- Guests use browser session storage; a browser's restore-session functionality may persist the session. Provide a manual clear control and backup download.
- Connected accounts are currently unavailable. Before activation, configure dedicated authentication, user-scoped access controls, data deletion/export, privacy notices, legal basis, retention periods and security procedures.
- No third-party tracking/advertising cookies should be added without an appropriate consent and compliance review.

## Source data
- Demo products are explicitly labelled. Reuse external product images, prices and stock only when legally authorised. Confirm availability at the supplier before accepting payment.
